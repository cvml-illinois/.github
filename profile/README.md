The [Computer Vision and Machine Learning Group](https://vision.ischool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://www.illinois.edu/) studies a wide range of problems in visual understanding, data-efficient learning, and intelligent perception. Our research addresses fundamental challenges in computer vision, machine learning, and multimodal learning, while also exploring real-world applications across domains such as healthcare and interactive systems.
